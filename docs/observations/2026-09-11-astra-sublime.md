## Both sides, then my choice

**For the tower as already planned:** it addresses the actual repeated cost: lost execution state, stale assignments, hand-carried obligations, self-referential manifests, resource conflicts and repeated reconstruction. Its vertical slices can retire real work before fleet infrastructure exists. Independent review already catches consequential false-green receipts. **Against calling that Sublime:** launch-to-terminal events omit requests never launched; a green candidate omits independent acceptance; a landed ratchet omits consumer uptake and the second encounter. A checksum does not stop a same-account worker from replacing the producer and evidence. Neither nine successful mechanisms nor three good blocks establishes the skills' denominator. My choice: preserve the build order, add explicit measurement/authority obligations to each block, and keep protected claims unknown until their external dependencies actually operate.

## 4. What the skills get wrong or overbuild

The twelve-step daily unit is the wrong runtime abstraction now. It mixes work, scheduled sampling, publication and weekly duties into a serial ceremony. Keep the obligations; let verbs execute ready work and a fold show what is due/owed. Daily close is a projection over continuing tasks, not a reset of their clocks or a quota to create work. Keep `prepare-day` visibly partial until its name/contract is reconciled.

A separate hand-maintained binding becomes another stale authority claim. Use one retained, content-addressed execution/policy manifest, referenced by `producer_epoch` and the assignment's authority/acceptance epoch. **Do not collapse those epochs into a bare producer version**: executable bytes do not encode independently ratified task selection or meaning. The plan-of-record envelope currently omits the `producer_epoch` present in my vision; restore it. Receipts and bindings should be views/references over that source, not duplicate mutable inventories.

The large receipt schema should be compositional: join immutable assignment, launch, check, acceptance, delivery and participation records. Require fields for each claimed scope; do not serialize irrelevant JVM facts for a prose consult. Keep the external trust boundary; drop repeated prose and duplicate state, not evidence obligations. Signed participation is justified only if its issuer/key and observation actually lie outside worker authority.

The skills' historical status is already self-contradictory: Sublime still calls binding/Andon unavailable; tighten has new rulings above old “unnamed” and “commit pending” paragraphs; CHANGE-RULE retains September 8 unappointed claims. This is itself a tower finding: decisions should supersede identified obligations, and generated runtime instructions should carry one current scoped contract. No automatic amendment of meaning by a text generator.

Do not make the board mechanically judge taste, expected protections, false refusals or which work matters. It should expose evidence and summon the named judgment. Likewise, replace indiscriminate repeated corpora with approved package-change/schedule/exposure triggers only through the change rule; preserve counterexamples and required cold proof. Making an inexpensive query is useful; building another grading hierarchy is not.
