## Taxonomy and receipt rule

The original taxonomy remains binding:

- **Grade C** — constructive kill: the mechanism is demonstrably the wrong subject.
- **Grade M** — measured with controls: the effect is absent on the tested stack.
- **Grade A** — arithmetic from the circular research corpus: a provisional grade that must be
  repriced on repository-stratified denominators.
- **Grade S** — screened against a preregistered threshold: the screen either seals the grave or
  promotes the mechanism.
- **Grade U** — unresolved: hold rather than kill.

Every empirical figure below is paired with its `branch@sha` receipt. The completed census
invalidates one global denominator: **the research-corpus-only 6.0% addressable ceiling is not a
production-repository ceiling**. External B has 119/266 established `src/`/`test/` actions,
44.74% of its native writes; External A has 0/63. External B also records four successful public
structural mutations. The sweep dispatch abbreviates adoption as `1.48% (4/266)`; the source
receipt defines the 1.48% rate as `4/(266+4)`, because 266 is the native-write count
(`experiment/external-corpus-shape-census@28ee81f4`, preregistered at
`experiment/external-corpus-shape-census@502f70a3`).

### Sweep 3 — native parse gate: Grade S, permanently sealed

This is the **strongest kill on the board**. A production window covered 114 sessions over 3.071
weeks and found three genuine loops, 0.98/week against the 15/week reopening bar. None of the
three was parser-catchable, and even the impossible all-eligible-write upper bound was only
10.42/week (`experiment/native-prelanding-gate-audit@e9d22019`).

Disposition: **PERMANENTLY SEALED under the native-write parse-gate claim.** A future parser idea
must enter as a different contract with a different benefit, not as another reopening cohort.

## Graves unchanged by the denominator correction

**Grade C remains closed:** positional line numbers, opaque mnemonic labels, and
counts-as-identity remain wrong-subject mechanisms. Reopening still requires independent effect
identity that binds label to owner and operation (`experiment/differential-routing-interview-20260829@8ac7cde`).

**Grade M remains closed on the tested stack:** catalog shrink, EDN carriage, format closure, and
copy-versus-compose retain their recorded controlled nulls and existing tripwires
(`experiment/differential-routing-interview-20260829@8ac7cde`). The census changes their market
denominator, not their mechanism evidence.

**Grade U remains unresolved:** EDN robustness still lacks a resolving cohort. Continuation
refusals were not tested by these sweeps and receive no promotion from the completeness result
(`experiment/differential-routing-interview-20260829@8ac7cde`).
