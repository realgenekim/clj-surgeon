A cheaper binding-preserving design to investigate is a **content-addressed immutable candidate snapshot**: hash its source/config/classpath manifest once when publishing the candidate, then reuse its Merkle root while probes load that exact immutable snapshot. Mutations must create a new snapshot; arbitrary mutable live trees cannot silently reuse the identity. A lossless change-journal-backed incremental manifest with full rehash on uncertainty is another candidate, but its invalidation proof must be established first. Neither was built here; replacing content binding with ordinary mtime/size equality is not the proposed optimization.
