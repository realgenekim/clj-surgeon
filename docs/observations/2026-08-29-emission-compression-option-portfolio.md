## Result

The strongest current performance mechanism is fewer emitted request bytes,
not less reasoning. The closed-relation result remains the champion. The best
new general option is `replacement_groups`, which states one exact
`from`/`to` decision once and lists its file/form sites.

1,189 of 6,409 compact JSON characters (18.55%), below the predeclared 20%
payload-reduction gate. Preserve the pure compiler witness and the cohort
protocol. Do not add a public grammar or spend model tokens until a frozen task
clears that gate.

## Why `replacement_groups` is the best new option

It removes repeated decisions rather than only repeated paths:

```text
FLAT

{file A, form f1, from X, to Y}
{file A, form f2, from X, to Y}
{file B, form f3, from X, to Y}

GROUPED

{from X, to Y,
 sites [{file A, forms [f1 f2]}
        {file B, forms [f3]}]}
```

The frozen task produces four groups, 12 grouped sites, and 21 retained unique
edits. The pure expansion reconstructs the same edit multiset. Generic file
groups save slightly less while preserving every repeated literal pair, and
they already have a retained correctness loss.

## Smallest compiler boundary

```text
public replacement_groups + ordinary edits
                    |
                    v
Phase A: pure, source-blind lowering
  - validate closed keys and types
  - require at least two sites per group
  - refuse duplicate decision keys
  - refuse duplicate file/form sites
  - refuse collisions with ordinary edits
  - expand deterministically to canonical edit rows
                    |
                    v
Phase B: existing canonical path
  public validation -> compact-location normalization
  -> one frozen snapshot -> one atomic transaction -> exact verifier
```

Phase A grants no file, owner, replacement, count, or verification authority.
Every site and exact decision remains caller-supplied. Phase B remains the only
source-capture and mutation engine. There must be no second transaction engine,
implicit owner lookup, fuzzy selection, or request-supplied executable policy.

Required red witnesses before any product work:

1. duplicate group decision;
2. duplicate site within or across groups;
3. grouped-site collision with an ordinary edit;
4. empty group, site, file, or form;
5. unknown key or invalid/nonpositive `matches`;
6. exact expansion to 33 rows, 37 matches, and nine files;
7. stale snapshot and wrong future hash refuse before write;
8. any one invalid member refuses the complete mixed transaction.

## Predeclared causal cohort

Do not launch this cohort on the current fixture. It first requires at least
20% predicted compact-argument reduction on a frozen, already-correct task.

When eligible, use one exact candidate, tool catalog, model/effort, task,
scorer generation, and server kernel. Both grammars remain visible in the same
tool surface. The arm instruction selects the representation; it does not add
task facts.

```text
Block 1: F G G F
Block 2: G F F G

F = canonical flat edits
G = replacement_groups plus only genuinely unique flat edits
```

Each run uses a fresh isolated workspace and process identity. Record every
attempt. Do not retry within a run.

Admission gates for every row:

- first actionable item is exactly one `apply_clojure_changes` call;
- zero prior inspect, shell, file, refusal, fallback, or recovery actions;
- route is adherent to the assigned shape;
- pure compilation yields 51 edits, nine files, and the exact frozen future;
- exact verifier passes and terminal evidence is complete;
- emitted compact arguments, output tokens, `T_emit`, server wall,
  result-to-final wall, and complete verified wall are finite and provenance
  bound.

Promotion gates:

- G is correct and route-adherent in all four positions;
- G emits fewer compact argument characters in both blocks and by at least 20%
  pooled;
- G lowers `T_emit` in both blocks and by at least 20% pooled;
- G lowers complete verified wall in both blocks and by at least 20% pooled;
- no position, refusal, or survivor-bias loss is omitted;
- an independent reversed eight-run cohort repeats the result before a
  headline or product recommendation.

If any arm is incorrect, compare correctness and route geometry only. Do not
publish a speed ratio. A prediction that fails is retained evidence.
