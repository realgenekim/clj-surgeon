## Phase gates (highest fully evidenced gate; pending never means passed)

Gene's phase names remain **rough → friction-high → friction-medium → friction-low → perfect**. **Uncalibrated** means capability without a witnessed comparative win; **suspended** means a performance route is withdrawn for its stated regime. Neither is a winning phase. A candidate is not a winning row waiting for polish.

**Integrity spine (Gene, 2026-09-08: "use LID skill to help ensure integrity"):** from friction-high upward, every fix registers stable EARS intent with code/test links (`INTENT:`/`INTENT-TEST:` and repository-recognized `@spec`) and a faithful fail-first witness. The actual `clj-surgeon.mcp-intent-contract/audit-current-repository` result is green in the round receipt. A retrospective link records the original red/green provenance; it does not invent a new red run. Open intent debt blocks medium; perfect requires zero verb debt.

- **rough** — a witnessed win against a native control, before the hardening contract is complete. Neither n≤2 nor absence of an oracle defines this phase.
- **friction-high** — output oracle and linked fix/witness ledger exist; ≥5 open friction items; rounds use the routine with its qualification status disclosed. Any unresolved critical defect blocks medium regardless of item count.
- **friction-medium** — 0–4 open friction items, zero critical defects, fixture oracle zero, intent audit green, reviewer GO, branch-trunk landing, and analysis/write/probe/proof timing decomposition. All receipts must be present. "Medium ceiling" is not certification that the audit is complete.
- **friction-low** — every medium gate, an eligible strictly-better route, complete receipt/escape/kill telemetry, four fully observed pilot cells, and two consecutive frozen-build timing reruns clearing the registered row threshold. Zero unresolved critical, safety or certification defects. Generic workflow success cannot replace the mutation route's own refusal and acceptance witnesses.
- **perfect** — every low gate, zero intent debt, two consecutive complete cold-start cohorts with zero forbidden attempts or operator intervention, apparatus share <50% of caller actions, automated fix → grade → review → land, doctrine and prompt plate delivery verified on every actual seat, and a calendar month of regression-free dogfood with eligible tasks. Add row-specific route/refusal/acceptance canaries and optional-choice evidence (at least three fresh fastest-safe choices) before calling a mutation route shipped. A month with zero eligible tasks is insufficient evidence.

The five-item hole is closed: high includes five, medium includes zero through four. Severity never reduces to ticket count. The workflow design's stricter proposed ≤25% apparatus/run budget is reported separately from the <50% perfection ceiling. Two complete cohorts mean the frozen 32-cell matrix twice, not any convenient sequence of 64 successes.
