# Squares revisited: the win is real, the route is narrower than the enthusiasm

**Committed answer.** Cell C establishes a genuine win for one exact shape: a large, fully mapped, single-source namespace partition whose static consequences and proof are compiled inside one task-shaped call. It does **not** establish that semantic tools generally beat native editing, that every namespace split now routes automatically, or that every operation can be made faster by wrapping it in a manifest. The durable discriminator is not “structural” versus “textual.” It is whether the operation removes a decision boundary the caller would otherwise have to cross.

Fable has the center of gravity right and three edges wrong:

1. The plan did not win merely by being data. Native and REPL arms had the same machine-ready manifest and still spent 90–296 seconds re-deriving the reference graph and constructing emitters. **Intent data plus a compiler that owns all derivable consequences** won.
2. The current informed fan-out schema does not deserve another run unchanged. Its direct paired test falsified it. A new cross-callsite migration that owns discovery, adjacent require/helper changes, application, and proof is a different square, not a retest of named-owner fan-out.
3. `namespace_split` is a win for the witnessed Curtain Call contract, but “joins the routed classes” is premature as a generalized class. The result reused old controls in later waves, has one fixture and one source shape, and has no free-choice cohort. It earns an exact-contract experimental route now and one cross-fixture replication before a general route.

The September 2 policy decision—native by default and no blanket “Surgeon expected” mandate—survives. Its universal explanation does not. “A standing 2x tax on every delegated Clojure job” must now be scoped to the old additive interface: ordinary/per-form workflows in which the caller keeps its native loop and layers Surgeon on top. Cell C is the counterexample to the unqualified sentence.

## 1. Attack on Fable's squares

The evidence boundary is important. Cell C's landed sequential runs were 56 seconds by CLI and 49 seconds by MCP, versus the unchanged native-with-manifest median of 447 seconds; the round-three four-run wave was 79–92 seconds with zero paper cuts. The earlier accepted verb runs were also far below native. That is not a variance-floor curiosity. It is a several-hundred-second effect, with correctness 4/4 and the paper-cut census driven to zero. ## 2. Candidate ranking

I use the requested score directly:

`opportunity = expected native complete-job wall W × expected eligible jobs F × transfer confidence C`.

`W` includes proportionate project verification but excludes benchmark-only mutation-oracle overhead; otherwise this ranking degenerates into “which repository has the slowest test suite.” `F` is an explicit planning prior per quarter across curtaincall-cfp, marvin-voice-remote, clj-surgeon, kiloclaw, and maven—not a claimed historical count. Four local histories were available and their subjects are too noisy to identify semantic refactors reliably; the kiloclaw working tree was not locally present. The numbers are intentionally falsifiable priors, not fake precision.

| Rank | Candidate | W (s) | F / quarter | C | W×F×C (weighted s/qtr) | Why the mechanism should transfer | One falsifier |
|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Cross-callsite API migration: add parameter, rename protocol method, retire Var | 360 | 12 | 0.78 | **3,370** | One declaration-level decision induces definitions, implementations, callers, requires, arity/dispatch checks, and proof. Static reference authority can remove a large site table. | On a frozen 10+-file/50+-site task, the caller must still enumerate or repair more than 10% of sites, or tool median fails to beat native by 30%. |
| 2 | Datastar route split | 420 | 7 | 0.82 | **2,411** | This is namespace partition plus route-table Var rewiring, handler requires, state ownership checks, and targeted tests; it closely resembles the witnessed compiler graph. | Pre-write analysis cannot account for every route-table/quoted-Var handler reference, or any arm passes structural checks while the independent route behavior oracle fails. |
| 3 | Move forms with dependencies, source retained | 300 | 8 | 0.92 | **2,208** | It is the closest strict subset of the winning split: one destination, the same owner/reference/promotion/declare/require/caller joins, less final-graph complexity. Cell B exposes exactly the missing boundary. | On the repaired Cell B fixture, the new one-call kernel does not reduce complete verified median by at least 30%, or any treated arm needs a native caller sweep. |
| 4 | Dependency upgrade as a migration manifest | 480 | 6 | 0.55 | **1,584** | A version decision can compile known namespace/API rewrites, config changes, deprecations, and checks as one guarded migration. | More than half of elapsed repair is dependency resolution/runtime incompatibility not representable by the manifest, and the route clears neither wall nor correctness threshold. |
| 5 | Test scaffolding from a manifest | 300 | 10 | 0.40 | **1,200** | A good manifest can derive namespaces, fixtures, test registration, repeated cases, and proof wiring. | Blind review finds that assertion intent still has to be invented or rewritten by the caller, or native one-patch generation is as fast with equal quality. |
| 6 | Namespace merge | 480 | 3 | 0.72 | **1,037** | It is the inverse graph projection: combine owners, resolve private/public status, rewrite callers, collapse requires, and prove one candidate. | Name/alias/metadata collisions produce more than one caller decision round per collision cluster or prevent failure-before-write on realistic fixtures. |
| 7 | Rename namespace with callers | 180 | 5 | 0.88 | **792** | Reference discovery, file/lib identity, require aliases, quoted Vars, callers, and proof are strongly reusable; an existing verb makes the build cheap. | A guarded native file move plus repository replace already completes under 90 seconds on two realistic fixtures, leaving no detectable wall for the verb to remove. |

This ranking is a portfolio ranking, not build order. Cross-callsite migrations have the largest expected fleet value, but `move-forms-with-deps` is the cheapest high-information next experiment because its kernel already exists and Cell B supplies a frozen task, native controls, and an adversarial oracle. Test scaffolding ranks surprisingly high only because it is frequent; its low transfer confidence says not to build it yet.

The deeper filter is simple. A candidate transfers when one user/model decision implies at least three independently checkable downstream consequences and the tool can authoritatively derive them on one snapshot. It does not transfer when the remaining work is mostly creative judgment, ecosystem debugging, or one patch the caller already knows how to express.

## 4. The one experiment to run next

Run **`move_forms_with_deps` versus native on the repaired Cell B calendar extraction**. This is more informative than another Curtain Call split replay: it tests transfer to a source-retained, one-destination operation, directly challenges the losing extraction interface, and exercises the proposed kernel beneath `namespace_split`.

### Pre-registration

**Fixture.** Freeze the Cell B base and task: move the named 25-form calendar/ICS set from `views.clj` to `cfp-scheduler-killer.exports.calendar`; source remains; caller table is not supplied; 43 structural caller sites across the admitted files must be discovered and rewritten. Use the existing A–E acceptance oracle only after fixing A8's case-sensitive `Unresolved symbol` defect and mutation-testing that fix. Freeze source, config, model, runner, cores, and prompt hashes.

**Arms.**

- **N — native fastest-safe:** task intent and 25-form set supplied; unrestricted native `rg`/scripts/patch; no Surgeon operation; normal project checks plus A–E.
- **V — whole-intent verb:** exactly one public mutation request carrying source, destination, form identities, admitted roots, visibility/alias policy, and proof profile. The split kernel must internally derive moved/retained dependencies, promotions, required declares, imports/requires, caller inventory, token rewrites, candidate, rollback, and named proof. No plan-only call, owner list, caller table, per-site edits, or native repair is allowed before the acceptance oracle. A typed pre-write unsupported result counts as a first-attempt failure and route loss, not a crash.

**n and schedule.** Run six identical N controls first to establish the current variance floor, then six N/V matched pairs in randomized lane order on the same pinned six-core allocations used by Cell B. Thus N has 12 observations total and V has 6; only the six contemporaneous matched N runs enter the paired effect, while the initial six define the floor. Fresh callers, one task per worktree, no shared analyzer cache unless both arms receive the same declared cache state.

**Primary clock.** `orient-start` through all A–E gates, including every request repair, fallback, suite, oracle, and caller completion. Also retain phase stamps and direct tool wall, but neither can replace the primary clock.

**Win threshold, fixed before the run.** V wins only if:

1. all 6/6 V runs and all included controls pass A–E with zero paper-cut regression;
2. V's median complete verified wall is at least **30% below** its matched N median and the absolute gap exceeds **2 sample SD** of the six-run native floor;
3. at least 5/6 V calls succeed on the first request, all 6/6 commit without native fallback, and there are zero unaccounted or dynamically discovered caller sites after the call;
4. blind diff review finds no greater unrelated churn than native; and
5. the caller authors no scanner, dependency graph, emitter, or caller-rewrite program. If it does, the proposed mechanism failed even if scheduling noise produces a fast wall.

If V misses only because the fixed A–E apparatus dominates wall, report that as a loss under the preregistration. The secondary work-phase clock may justify redesigning the oracle, but it may not be substituted for the result.

Why not fan-out next? Because its existing hypothesis has already been answered. Why not cross-callsite API migration first despite its higher opportunity score? Because move-forms is a near-isomorphic transfer, the compiler exists, and Cell B makes the next bit of evidence much cheaper. If this strict subset cannot win, stop generalizing the split mechanism and revisit the causal story before building the larger candidates.
