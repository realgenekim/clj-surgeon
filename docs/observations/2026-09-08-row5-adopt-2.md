terms: the fact's own declared `:excludes` (strings, semicolon prose, dynamic resolution) name

**2. The `:proof` block killed arm E's unanimous foot-gun.** In arm E all six reports named

**3. Exposing raw vs authorized-replay hashes as two columns is the right call.** Four arms

## The losses

**1. THE PRIMARY GATE FAILED, and one arm is why.** Per-slot ratios 1.00, 0.62, 0.38, **1.80**,
1.00, 0.42. Two arms were already at E's floor of 5 and could not fall. One arm, X4, went 10 →
**18**: it accepted every negative *and then independently re-derived them* — a form-by-form
body diff of all 25 owners against base bytes, a reflective visibility/arity check in a live
JVM, two fresh-process loads. **A verifiable negative invites verification.** Excluding X4 the
median paired ratio is 0.615 — still above 0.50. Disclosed sensitivity, not a gate change.

**2. `:comment_edits` ships a defect, and 6/6 callers found the same one independently.** The
receipt emits a first group with `:after_file nil` whose single row is
`[1822 nil "    ;; every live legacy key has been rotated; new facts never take it." nil]`.
Under the receipt's own declared `:columns [:line :after_line :before :after]` that reads as
*this comment was deleted*. It was not: the line survives verbatim at 1465 of the mutated file
(1822 − 357 shifted lines), 750 lines from the calendar block, with no diff hunk near it. The
second group then emits ~30 rows pairing each before-line with the text at the shifted
after-line, so untouched comments appear rewritten into their neighbours; two rows describe
the one real edit. Six independent diagnoses, same shape: "a shifted zip", "positional
alignment, not a diff", "off-by-one pairing", "an alignment artifact emitted in the same shape
as a deletion". **Every arm fell back to reading `git diff` by hand to confirm the comment
policy.** The one fact whose job is to bound the comment change is the one fact that returns
the by-hand work it exists to remove.

**3. Wall got worse: paired 1.33, median 121.5 s → 156.6 s.** Two structural causes, both
disclosed in advance: the brief grew by the negatives section and the receipt grew ~23 KB →
36.5 KB, and reading it is caller work inside the clock. E2 still runs at 0.53 of the native
floor, so the row is far ahead of native; it is behind its own previous build.

**4. Callers miscount a count the receipt prints.** `:bodies_preserved` carries
`:raw_equal 19 :raw_unequal 6` in all six receipts with an identical owner set. X1 and X4 said
six; **X2 and X5 confidently said four, X3 said three, X6 said "four … and others."** Not a
misunderstanding under the preregistered definition (which scopes to what moved / caller files
/ sites / promoted / retained — 0/6 there), so the gate is unaffected. Recorded because it is
real: callers counted rows by eye instead of reading the summary field two lines away.

## Learnings → ratchets

1. **A negative fact must be a diff, never a positional alignment — and an unaligned surviving
   line must never encode as a deletion.** Ratchet: a witness that runs the comment policy on a
   source whose net line count shrinks, asserting (a) rows are emitted only where before-text ≠
   after-text at the mapped position, and (b) no row carries `:after nil` for a line still
   present in the candidate. This is the highest rung available short of a typed refusal: the
   receipt currently makes a false statement about the tree it just wrote.
2. **Put the headline number where the reader lands.** Four of six arms misread a printed
   count because they tallied rows instead of reading `:raw_unequal`. Ratchet: every negative
   whose evidence is a table states its summary count in the line immediately before the table,
   and a receipt-shape test asserts summary-before-rows for all five negatives.
3. **"Fewer inspections" is the wrong success metric for a verifiable claim.** The arm that
   trusted the negatives least (X4) also produced the most thorough acceptance, first attempt,
   and its extra work was corroboration of facts the receipt volunteered. Ratchet for the next
   cohort: preregister *inspections the receipt could have answered* separately from
   *corroborations of a receipt claim*; the first is friction, the second may be a feature.
4. **The `:proof` block is a shipped, measured win — 6/6 correct readings of a field that
   confused 6/6 in the previous build.** It should be treated as the template for every future
   status field that can read as failure while being correct.

## What's next

  failed, it failed identically in six independent hands, and it is the one every arm named as
  the reason they still read the diff. A repeat of E2 on the fix is the cheapest available test
  of whether the paired ratio moves; the apparatus is frozen and reusable byte-for-byte.
- **Do not re-run E, D or N.** Their controls are frozen and were reused here.
- Open question for the next preregistration: whether the gate should be paired-ratio at all,
  given two arms sat at the floor in both builds. A floor-aware gate (median arms at or below
  5 postcommit inspections) would have passed on this data; that must be declared before the
