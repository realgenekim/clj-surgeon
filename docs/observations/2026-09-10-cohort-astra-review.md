# Astra review: programs-to-contracts cohort

**Decision: run with the eight exact edits below, as a pilot.** Do not advertise
this as a powered safety study or as admission of new automatic routing classes.
“Commit” here is my recommendation; this consult changes only this review file.

The proposal is useful: matched tasks, fresh sessions, two callers, independent
oracles and observed method choice can test an intervention. Its current headline
confuses compliance with improvement, and its three arms cannot separate the
exception from the plate's effect when the exception is absent.

**1. What Gene should read first**

Read **the percentage-point increase in independently accepted completions by a
fixed deadline, AC minus B**, with counts, paired uncertainty and caller breakdown.
Define acceptance as intended behavior plus preservation plus required gates.
Use equal caller and T/R/I weights for the one summary; label that target explicitly.
This is improvement on this task distribution, not improvement across the census.

It becomes a lie if timeouts/refusals disappear from the denominator, oracle failures
are repaired with private hints in one arm, the oracle accepts only the builder's
preferred spelling, or cherry-picked easy tasks stand for all Clojure work. An
aggregate can also hide a regression in one caller. B must be an attested historical
prompt, not a newly exaggerated instruction to use Python.

The strongest rival headline is reduction in **oracle-fail AND gates-green** outcomes.
That captures E4's actual danger. But alone it rewards an arm that fails every gate
or never completes. Report that joint rate per assigned task immediately beside
acceptance, plus the conditional rate among green-gate runs with its denominator.
Program share is a mechanism measure: replacing a correct Python edit with an
incorrect Edit call is compliance and deterioration simultaneously.

**2. Attack the arms and sample size**

For B: the shell preference really existed, so keeping it is fair for measuring the
proposed policy change in that harness. Against B: only 107/743 census sessions
carried it; with it, the reported rates were Sonnet 30% and Opus 97%, versus 15% and
89% without it. The pooled 17%/90% are neither forecasts nor matched controls.
The census includes mandated benchmark arms, generation, repeated sessions and
coarse intent labels. It cannot establish a causal model trait or a defect rate.

Add **C = B + plate, without exception**. B/A/AC estimates exception effect without
plate and plate effect with exception; it cannot estimate plate effect without
exception or their interaction. C costs 24 runs and resolves that ambiguity.
Also add a **competent native N**: exact Edit/Write/apply_patch, batched when useful,
ordinary discovery and equal verification. B is not this control. A may choose
Surgeon, so A cannot substitute for it. N tests whether any gain needs Surgeon.

“Free choice; never the tool” contradicts AC naming verbs and routing automatically.
Call A/C/AC policy treatments with choices left inside their permitted routes.
Do not infer voluntary adoption from compliance with a routing mandate.

Twelve paired tasks are a pilot, not twelve independent observations of every call.
One run moves the cell rate 8.3 points; each intent has only four observations.
At 17%, expect about two program-using tasks if that rate applied; at 3%, less than
one. Even 0 failures/12 leaves an exact one-sided 95% upper bound near 22%.
Illustrative independent-Bernoulli planning at two-sided 5%, 80% power needs roughly
71/arm for 17%→3%, 62 for 90%→70%, and 19 for 90%→50%. These are normal-approximation
scale checks, not a powered design for paired, heterogeneous tasks. Pairing can help;
the unknown discordance and session variation determine how much. Wilson intervals
on separate cells do not quantify the paired treatment difference.

**Least-sure judgment:** a very large Opus method shift is plausible enough to justify
this small pilot. Its size is not established, and fresh short tasks may erase the
historical baseline before either intervention acts. Do not promise any “→ ?” value.

**3. Attack the task draw**

Four per family is useful coverage, not census weighting. The broad counts sum to
4,424/6,124 = **72.2%**, not ~80%; insertion after a defn is only a subset of the third
category, and test-body edits mix replacement with adding tests. The 60-row table is
explicitly ordered by breakage, blast radius and absent verification. Randomly drawing
from it removes final chooser discretion, but cannot remove its failure enrichment.

Use the census's full hit list, with an independent curator checking actual intents
and historical pre-edit snapshots, then a public seeded draw. Keep rejected rows,
reasons and deterministic reserves. The verb builder must not choose examples,
rewrite hard tasks into easy ones, or exclude unsupported contracts. All arms get
the identical task and initial bytes. If only the 60 rows can be reconstructed,
use the same drawing rule but label the result a **failure-enriched challenge pilot**.
It cannot estimate field improvement. Transplanting everything into curtaincall-cfp
would further turn historical episodes into synthetic analogues; retain original
repositories where reconstructable. Never silently call an analogue a replay.

**4. Meter and complete wall**

Keep complete wall in the first results table, not the appendix, but not as the
single verdict or a score blended with correctness. Gene permits a modest premium
for relevant protection; he did not make all delays costless. “Strictly better, or
native” still requires matched native cost evidence for performance routing.
A safety win at greater cost may justify an explicit safety-policy decision; it
must not be relabeled a demonstrated speed win.

E4's displayed “complete elapsed” ends at oracle verdict and separately lists about
19 seconds of repo tests. Do not copy that boundary as complete acceptance cost.
Measure through all required gates, review, repair and independent acceptance;
include queueing. Freeze the first submitted result before any oracle feedback.
For failures, report time spent and deadline status, not a fictitious success time.
Successful-only wall would select different tasks in different arms.

Record task-level program use, first method and the full method sequence, including
fallback. The census counts calls and puts git apply in “program”; a request-file
heredoc is not a Clojure mutation. Preserve its call-share column for comparison,
but do not let extra editor calls mechanically manufacture a program-share win.

**5. Remaining confounds**

- Context: freeze prompt ordering, history and task information. Plate token cost is
  part of its delivered effect. Fresh-session findings need a later long-context
  replication; do not claim this pilot isolates the model's enduring habits.
- Model settings: pin resolved model/version, runner, effort, token budget and all
  exposed sampling settings. Record temperature/seed as unavailable when hidden;
  subscription access is not evidence of deterministic or identical backends.
- Documentation: hold tools, descriptions, skills and help text fixed across B/A/C/AC.
  Hash the actually loaded global/project instructions: today's global file already
  contains the exception and the older routing plate. Merely omitting a line from
  the task prompt does not create B or C. Keep prior E4 answers and oracle internals
  out of caller context; the task may state preservation requirements equally.
- Freshness: hash base files, configuration and untracked inputs. A fresh worktree
  does not reset HOME instructions, caches, semantic state or dependency downloads.
  Freeze versions throughout; balance cache warming and execution order across arms.
- Oracle dependence: rewrite-clj under a different wrapper is a shared failure mode.
  The proposal's reported 10/10 bracket-hasher agreement is narrow evidence, not
  general independence. Use raw-byte preservation plus separately derived semantic
  assertions; test comments, discards, strings and changed-owner collateral damage.
  A multiset can miss strings swapped between locations. Reject wrong behavior that
  remains parseable, and accept multiple genuinely correct implementations.
- Interference: four concurrent runs share CPU, subscription limits and serialized
  kondo. Randomize balanced task/caller blocks; record throttling and queue delay.
  Freeze formatting and verification policy so churn is not an arm-specific tax.
- Evaluation: blind graders to arm, classify refusals as warranted/unwarranted/unknown,
  and retain grader disagreement. A refusal followed by native success is treatment
  success with fallback, not missing data; an oracle crash is unknown, not PASS.

**6. Exact edits to proposal.md — eight changes**
