# Lessons, each tied to at least two ledger rows

1. **Measure the clock before optimising the tool.** Row 5 found 87% of wall is model time and 3 to 4% is tool time; rows 4 and 3 (per-form writes, fan-out) then lost against native exactly as that composition predicts, because each tool call costs a model return and saves only tool time. Everything that won (rows 1, 2) removed model returns, not tool seconds.
2. **A win is a whole intent replaced by one call, nothing smaller.** Rows 1 and 2 (namespace split, alias migration) each replace a many-action native task with one call and won; rows 3 and 4 wrapped single edits and lost. The session data agrees: the native candidates that clear the five-action bar are add-require-across-files (median 29 actions), extract-function (16), rename-namespace (20) and alias migration (14.5) (`candidates.md`).
3. **Refusals are the product agents actually met.** In 2,049 calls, 220 refusals and 86 errors against 1,172 successes; fallback to native after 24% of all calls and 35% of MCP calls (`fallback_by_op.tsv`); row 9's admission gate refused 36 of 74 calls. Rows 7 and 9 show the same thing from the inside: the gate and the plate were built faster than the refusals were studied. The papercut list (57 entries) covers the output of one verb and none of the twenty most frequent friction patterns (`friction_top20.md`).
4. **Unmeasured effort stacks.** Rows 8, 12 and 14 together carry roughly 2,800 to 2,900 unique commits each on stacked branches and no wall receipt; the machine ledger finds 423 never-measured and 772 inconclusive series against 2 won and 4 genuinely lost (`ledger.tsv`). Of 5,994 commits, 1,396 are on main and 4,598 are on branches, almost all between 2026-09-02 and 2026-09-24.
5. **Review verdicts are not measurements.** Rows 10 and 11 end in NO-GO, GO-WITH-FIX and "saves zero returns" from Sol and Opus reviews; none has a native control. A review round costs a day of commits (rows 11's siblings each sit at about 2,560 commits) and settles nothing a pair of timed runs would not settle faster.
6. **Usage stopped when the measuring stopped.** Every one of the 2,049 surgeon calls on Anvil falls between 2026-09-02 and 2026-09-15 (`calls_per_week.tsv`): 1,531 the first week, 459 the second, 59 the third, then none. Rows 1 and 2 are the only routes that survived into doctrine, and no session here has exercised them since. A routed verb nobody calls has the same value as an unmeasured one.
7. **Branch sprawl is a cost of its own.** 554 branches, 38 sibling working copies, 14,880 generated observation files in one series (row 8), and 2,788 commits that cannot be assigned to a single verdict (`commits_by_verdict.tsv`). Rows 8, 11 and 14 could not be scored because their receipts, if they exist, are not findable from the branch.
