Receipt-silent means that at least one task-relevant proposition the action is actually used to establish has no adequate answer in the complete receipt available before that action. What can retire trust-witness? My committed choice is independent observation through the recorder boundary, for the claims it actually witnesses. Not merely a recorder UID, and not merely a signed Surgeon receipt. Z6’s cleanest formulation is “It is a second read, not a second witness.” Its concern about status is “the one claim where a self-report and a repair are indistinguishable if the tool were wrong.” A tool-executed :worktree_clean line remains the same issuer’s account of itself. E4 already contains the essential executed git-status fact, and the callers still checked. Promoting it into :facts or rendering changed paths plainly may move Z5’s presentation-driven arm; I do not predict that it moves an arm whose stated requirement is evidence outside Surgeon’s chain.

Moreover, an ordinarily interpreted :worktree_clean true would be false in this experiment: the intended split leaves ten modified files and a new destination. The meaningful proposition is no changes outside the authorized footprint and declared apparatus, at a named snapshot. A single boolean that conflates those states would worsen the receipt. An executed status fact after apparatus removal could close the real temporal gap, but if Surgeon or its caller only reports that execution, it still has not supplied the independent witness Z1, Z3 and Z6 describe. Relabeling that action from receipt-silent to trust-witness while it continues to occur is a coverage improvement and zero action savings.
