# Adaptive Performance Regression Sentinel Requirements

These requirements define the first Sessionize extraction sentinel. Status
`[ ]` marks a specified, unimplemented gap that requires a direct red witness.
Sentinel evidence is deliberately more sensitive than promotion evidence and
can never satisfy or weaken a speed-promotion gate.

## Closed Request and Product Identity

- [ ] **PERF-SENT-CONFIG-001**: When the sentinel decodes a request, it shall accept only the closed versioned request fields and modes, derive result, archive, and ledger roots from controller policy, and refuse unknown, missing, mutable-ref, caller-root, or caller-policy input before authentication, product materialization, or model launch; the derived outer result shall be absent or empty, canonical, nonsymlinked, directory-valued, and confined, and shall refuse a symlink, canonical alias escape, nonempty path, or regular file.
- [ ] **PERF-SENT-IDENT-001**: Before each attempt and after it completes, the sentinel shall prove the exact stable tag object when annotated, peeled stable commit and tree, candidate commit and tree, controller commit and tree, worker and policy hashes, artifact-manifest hash, materializer-receipt hash, canonical materialized root, and exact pre/post path inventory; any drift or unallowlisted generated path shall invalidate the invocation without using `git status` as archive cleanliness authority.
- [ ] **PERF-SENT-IDENT-002**: When one versioned product arm runs, both the MCP server and client-registry projection shall resolve product source from that arm's one materialized root while the controller continues to own the fixture, prompt, observer, scorer, clock, schedule, and retention policy; a split product root or product-owned scorer shall invalidate the invocation, and ordinary `run_clean_codex` outside sentinel mode shall preserve its existing controller-root default.
- [ ] **PERF-SENT-CLIENT-001**: Before model launch, the sentinel shall freeze canonical path, SHA-256, and version evidence for Codex, its package and platform package, native executable, Node, operating system, architecture, model, reasoning level, tool-selection policy, fixture tree, capsule, task, rendered prompt, expected ordered source manifest, exact profile bytes, host admission policy, and resolved formatter and verifier; it shall require every declared arm-independent identity and per-attempt resolved runtime identity to remain equal across all attempts.
- [ ] **PERF-SENT-SURFACE-001**: Before each model attempt, the sentinel shall capture the complete advertised catalog and canonical client projection, expose exactly the common apply-only route, charge each arm its own apply description and schema, and require exact same-arm surface repetition only when that arm has a second position.
- [ ] **PERF-SENT-AUTH-001**: Before remote work, Skiff shall issue under one short ledger lock a controller-authenticated expiring launch-admission receipt binding invocation, current head and state, mode, scope, stable, blocked candidate when any, accepted candidate, supersession, owner authorization, nonce, and controller identity, and shall release that lock before remote model work.
- [ ] **PERF-SENT-AUTH-002**: Before authentication or model launch, dev-a shall validate the exact launch-admission receipt, controller authentication, invocation-manifest hash, nonce, expiry, and bound identities without reading or writing the Skiff ledger.
- [ ] **PERF-SENT-AUTH-003**: Before final verdict or append, Skiff shall revalidate current state and the admitted identities under the append lock; launch admission shall grant no append, clear, projection, or publication authority, and concurrent state drift shall fail closed.

## Admission and Attempt Evidence

- [ ] **PERF-SENT-ADMIT-001**: Immediately before every C1, S1, S2, or C2 attempt in the raw event order, the pressure sampler shall emit one distinct complete observation and admission decision under the frozen policy and maximum age, and shall never launch, queue, or kill benchmark work.
- [ ] **PERF-SENT-ADMIT-002**: Before each attempt, the coordinator shall allocate distinct workspace, home, port, and result-root identities and shall launch only after accepting that attempt's fresh admitted pressure receipt; stale, incomplete, rejected, reused, or drifted admission or resource evidence shall launch no attempt.
- [ ] **PERF-SENT-ATTEMPT-001**: When attempt evidence is compiled, it shall contain one unique turn ID and one unique apply call ID joined exactly across start, completion, capture, result, and receipt records; the apply shall be the first actionable item, no other message, tool, shell, or file action may precede or accompany it, every record shall be consumed once, and duplicate, orphan, extra, reversed, or mismatched records shall invalidate the invocation.
- [ ] **PERF-SENT-ATTEMPT-002**: While the temporary workspace exists, the controller-owned scorer shall emit one closed semantic receipt binding its schema and code hash, scorer inputs, expected ordered source manifest, final ordered source manifest, and exact source set before workspace cleanup.
- [ ] **PERF-SENT-ATTEMPT-003**: When an attempt is later compiled, the pure compiler shall validate the closed semantic receipt plus raw route, read-back, and verifier evidence and shall refuse tampering, omission, or disagreement rather than trust summary booleans, caller aggregates, or claim to rerun a scorer after workspace deletion.
- [ ] **PERF-SENT-TIME-001**: When one attempt is valid, `T_verified` shall be the positive integer interval from observed turn start through observed turn completion with strict `turn start < apply start < apply completion < turn completion`, and the joined apply completion shall carry exact verification, read-back, result, and receipt evidence for that call; server elapsed, process wall, formatter time, verifier phase time, and presentation strings shall remain diagnostic only.

## Adaptive Schedule and Thresholds

- [ ] **PERF-SENT-SCHEDULE-001**: Given valid C1 and S1 values, pure `compile-screen-decision` shall return green-stop only when `100*C1 < 108*S1` and reverse-required when `100*C1 >= 108*S1`, without launching a process or accepting rounded presentation values.
- [ ] **PERF-SENT-SCHEDULE-002**: Given the pure screen decision, the coordinator shall execute exactly C1 then S1 for green-stop or exactly C1, S1, S2, C2 for reverse-required, serially with the frozen one-worker, one-replica, model, reasoning, task, profile, and tool policy.
- [ ] **PERF-SENT-SCHEDULE-003**: When imported evidence is compiled, the sentinel shall refuse an extra reverse pair after green, a missing reverse attempt after a trigger, or any reordered, duplicated, or unrecognized position as schedule-violation evidence.
- [ ] **PERF-SENT-SCHEDULE-004**: Every launched attempt, including losing and invalid attempts, shall remain in the invocation evidence in launch order; a child, observer, scorer, identity-fence, or coordinator failure shall propagate nonzero, and an infrastructure retry shall use a new invocation ID without dropping, replacing, or overwriting an attempt.
- [ ] **PERF-SENT-THRESHOLD-001**: The sentinel shall use positive integer cross-products only; exactly eight percent shall trigger, exactly ten percent may confirm red only when `C1>S1` and `C2>S2`, equal walls shall not count as a positional loss, and binary floating point or rounded presentation values shall grant no verdict authority.

## Typed Verdicts and Publication

- [ ] **PERF-SENT-VERDICT-001**: When closed evidence is compiled against current ledger state, the sentinel shall emit exactly one legal green, yellow, red, recovered, or invalid variant with its required schedule, metrics, reason, publication state, required exit, `promotion_authority=false`, and no inapplicable fields; an impossible status/metric/mode/publication combination shall refuse.
- [ ] **PERF-SENT-VERDICT-002**: Given a valid triggered four-attempt screen, the sentinel shall classify red only when `C1>S1`, `C2>S2`, and `100*(C1+C2) >= 110*(S1+S2)`; every other valid triggered screen shall be yellow.
- [ ] **PERF-SENT-VERDICT-003**: The closed non-invalid reason enum shall bind green only to `:below-trigger`, ordinary yellow only to `:triggered-not-confirmed`, recovery yellow only to `:recovery-not-cleared`, red only to `:confirmed-regression`, and recovered only to `:recovery-proved`; any other reason or status/mode/reason combination shall refuse.
- [ ] **PERF-SENT-INVALID-001**: If an invocation is invalid, the sentinel shall use exactly one of `:candidate-correctness-failure`, `:stable-baseline-failure`, `:invalid-environment`, `:invalid-evidence`, `:identity-drift`, `:schedule-violation`, `:not-comparable`, or `:incomplete-archive-input`, shall carry no slowdown metric or confirmed-regression label, and shall exit nonzero; invalid environment and broken evidence shall never be named or projected as a product slowdown.
- [ ] **PERF-SENT-INVALID-002**: If retention, import, ledger append, projection, or another coordinator boundary fails, the coordinator shall use exactly one of `:retention-failure`, `:import-failure`, `:ledger-append-failure`, `:projection-failure`, or `:coordinator-boundary-failure` and exit nonzero; pre-authority retention, import, or append failure shall create no externally usable ledger verdict, while post-authority projection failure shall preserve the already-appended event and ledger state, and neither shall be fabricated or relabeled as invalid product evidence or regression.
- [ ] **PERF-SENT-PUBLISH-001**: When prepublication measurement is green or yellow, publication shall be allowed only if the resulting applicable ledger state is clear and candidate identity remains exact; an already-open or concurrently opened red shall keep publication blocked with the original red event ID, and only authorized recovered evidence may clear it.
- [ ] **PERF-SENT-PUBLISH-002**: The sentinel shall publish prepublication green or yellow as allowed only from resulting clear state, recovery recovered as allowed only for the exact authorized candidate, recovery yellow, red, or invalid as blocked with the prior red, and nightly or backfill as not applicable; the public terminal receipt shall exit zero for prepublication or recovery only when publication is allowed, use measurement-status exit for nightly or backfill, and exit nonzero for any later lifecycle failure, while no condition may make red or invalid zero.
- [ ] **PERF-SENT-PROMOTION-001**: Every sentinel verdict shall use a sentinel-specific schema and invocation ID and shall carry `promotion_authority=false`; events, archives, and publication receipts shall bind that exact verdict and schema, and no sentinel result, repeated nightly, native denominator, or faster backfill may emit a speedup-earned result, update a promotion receipt, satisfy a promotion gate, contribute a row to a promotion cohort, or advance the stable baseline.
- [ ] **PERF-SENT-PROMOTION-002**: If the closed sentinel decoder receives promotion fields or a request to satisfy a promotion gate, or if the promotion verifier receives sentinel evidence where promotion evidence is required, that decoder or verifier shall refuse with type `:sentinel-promotion-authority-refused` before model launch, ledger append, promotion-record mutation, or baseline mutation, while preserving existing sentinel evidence unchanged.

## Skiff Ledger and Projections

- [ ] **PERF-SENT-LEDGER-001**: Skiff alone shall own final verdict and ledger authority; under one short local lock it shall read and validate the current chain, compile a chain-independent event body from imported evidence and current state, wrap it with the current prior-event hash, append the immutable envelope once, and treat chain files rather than the mutable head cache as authority.
- [ ] **PERF-SENT-LEDGER-002**: Under the ledger lock, an absent invocation ID may append once, an existing identical invocation shall return its original append receipt and replay only incomplete projections, and an existing invocation with different evidence, body, envelope, or state shall fail nonzero; crash reconciliation shall repair only a stale head cache and shall never append a second event.
- [ ] **PERF-SENT-LEDGER-003**: The ledger shall apply exactly these transitions and append their event: clear plus green, yellow, or invalid remains clear; clear plus red becomes blocked by that red; blocked plus green, yellow, invalid, or another red remains blocked by the original red; and blocked plus authorized recovered becomes clear; no issue, Director state, or acknowledgement shall mutate the ledger, and no ordinary green, yellow, invalid, or red screen shall clear an existing block.
- [ ] **PERF-SENT-LEDGER-004**: If import or append fails, no externally usable ledger verdict shall exist; retained evidence and the terminal failure receipt shall remain available for boundary reconciliation under the same invocation without another model cohort.
- [ ] **PERF-SENT-LEDGER-ROOT-001**: The Skiff ledger root shall come only from hashed controller configuration, resolve to one canonical nonsymlink directory, and refuse `/`, the product checkout, workspace, result root, archive root, or any canonical alias of them before ledger read or lock acquisition.
- [ ] **PERF-SENT-PROJECT-001**: After an authoritative event, issue and Director projections shall be idempotent consequences keyed by event ID; red shall own one blocking issue, yellow one distinct nonblocking issue, green or recovered shall close superseded yellow attention, invalid shall clear nothing, and projection failure shall leave ledger state unchanged and final process exit nonzero until replay succeeds.

## Recovery and Boundary Reconciliation

- [ ] **PERF-SENT-RECOVERY-001**: For recovery mode, the launch-admission receipt shall additionally require one exact open red and bind its scope, stable, blocked candidate, accepted same or explicitly superseding candidate, and allowlisted owner authorization; a fabricated, closed, mismatched, or expired red shall refuse before remote work.
- [ ] **PERF-SENT-RECOVERY-002**: Given accepted recovery launch authority, the coordinator shall execute exactly fresh C1, S1, S2, C2 attempts with per-attempt admission and shall never use the adaptive early stop.
- [ ] **PERF-SENT-RECOVERY-004**: Given one valid four-attempt recovery, the pure compiler shall classify recovered only when `100*C1 < 108*S1`, `100*C2 < 108*S2`, and `100*(C1+C2) < 108*(S1+S2)`; every other valid recovery shall be yellow or red under the closed verdict law, and invalid evidence shall remain invalid.
- [ ] **PERF-SENT-RECOVERY-003**: When recovery identities are decoded, nested stable and scope shall equal top-level and referenced-red identities, a same candidate shall equal the blocked and top-level candidate, a replacement shall equal the top-level candidate and explicitly supersede the blocked candidate, and owner authorization shall bind all of them; any mismatch, acknowledgement, issue closure, Director state, ordinary green, or inferred Git ancestry shall grant no clear authority.
- [ ] **PERF-SENT-RECONCILE-001**: If import, append, or projection fails after remote retention, publication-boundary reconciliation may replay only the same retained evidence, event, append, or projection under the same invocation identity without another model cohort or a second retention; it shall never change sentinel status, produce recovered, clear a red, or invent a replacement attempt.

## Retention, Import, Release, Baseline, and Backfill

- [ ] **PERF-SENT-RETAIN-001**: The exact controller checkout on dev-a shall derive one empty confined outer result path, retain the complete invocation exactly once under the existing archive contract, and preserve every raw attempt, materializer, environment, schedule, semantic, and remote-execution receipt; child attempts shall not produce independently retained archives.
- [ ] **PERF-SENT-IMPORT-001**: Before local verdict compilation, Skiff import shall verify the exact transfer manifest, immutable archive, structured manifest, archive receipt, remote-execution receipt, and confined temporary extraction without trusting an unretained remote result tree.
- [ ] **PERF-SENT-IMPORT-002**: After import, Skiff shall independently recompile every attempt and the adaptive schedule from imported raw evidence and require exact equality with the dev-a attempt evidence, screen decision, and schedule before ledger access.
- [ ] **PERF-SENT-RECEIPT-001**: Only after successful import, final verdict, ledger append, and every required projection shall Skiff write a publication receipt binding remote execution, retained evidence, archive, final verdict, event body, event envelope, append, and projection identities; otherwise it shall write only the corresponding closed terminal-failure receipt.
- [ ] **PERF-SENT-RECEIPT-002**: The publication receipt shall live outside the immutable archive and bind its SHA-256 and structured-manifest SHA-256 without requiring an object inside the archive to contain the archive's own future hash.
- [ ] **PERF-SENT-RELEASE-001**: After cutover, `make install-verified` shall run deterministic `verify-performance-regression-release` immediately before low-level installation and shall refuse missing, invalid, red, projection-incomplete, candidate-mismatched, or applicable-open-red evidence; low-level `make install` alone shall remain an emergency nonpublication artifact install and shall not create a stable-publication claim, and install or reload shall never launch Anvil, a model, or the sentinel.
- [ ] **PERF-SENT-BASELINE-001**: The stable manifest shall bind an immutable tag literal, annotated tag-object hash when present, peeled commit, tree, route, nominating owner, and nomination receipt; the sentinel runner shall never infer, write, or automatically advance that baseline.
- [ ] **PERF-SENT-BACKFILL-001**: Historical backfill shall compare candidate `75585beeda63a4dcc9bb1e219d5721d89b93baa2` tree `b61fe3610643ddd23f1e7061879ac871b55e623a` against stable `b8e52cb603c35471cab6d4f562161a1a588c3b20` tree `fe42cf35db2c743bd64351fab65f03f63686034e`, then candidate `19ab864889799b0028a5f7cb66c63b957ff7b973` tree `72e1ef5fc09587013e7b8d60f2ed027385280973` against stable `75585beeda63a4dcc9bb1e219d5721d89b93baa2`, under fixture tree `b0a3578f0cfc310afe17a4201a8a6095a057f070`, task blob `58dee06dfef9bf41b7ca26845d07380436354d9a`, capsule blob `a91227ae77a1618eae94f68cd7b7c039064fd619`, exact-profile SHA-256 `9976206370ed858dbe0831928e4894f084a779af4f6243485fefdde1fc4e17ac`, prompt SHA-256 `c2e7cdc2c4e3c88aa6b00d395073dade0d38e89ee45a41876892a525f17c3ed1`, and overlay SHA-256 `7ec3381861c53053ce98802f0c038b05709843bc6453b2e6e59f9eeb048fd6c7`; it shall create fresh evidence for every position without treating Git ancestry as install order or reusing `75585be` evidence, and shall report an unsupported common route as not comparable without synthetic timing or publication authority.
- [ ] **PERF-SENT-CUTOVER-001**: The deterministic cutover validator shall accept only a closed retained acceptance receipt proving no-model contract coverage plus the complete required external Anvil receipt, and shall refuse missing, mismatched, partial, or sentinel-promotion evidence without launching a model.
- [ ] **PERF-SENT-CUTOVER-002**: Before the sentinel gates stable publication, one complete model-bearing Anvil replay shall externally prove attempt joins, adaptive stop, reverse scheduling, invalid typing, retention/import, ledger append, projection, exit behavior, and promotion refusal; that external receipt is release evidence rather than a unit-test substitute.
- [ ] **PERF-SENT-CUTOVER-003**: The explicit rollback command shall remove only the publication prerequisite and scheduled trigger and shall preserve the adaptive evidence schedule, retained evidence, ledger events, projections, and prior red state; its deterministic witness shall prove no broader deletion or product-runtime change.
